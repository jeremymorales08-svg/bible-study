Amplified Bible (AMP) and Spanish versions

The Amplified Bible and modern Spanish versions (RVR1960, NVI…) are copyrighted, so they load through API.bible with a free API key. Spanish without a key is already available: Reina-Valera 1909, in the translation dropdown.

  1. Create a free key at scripture.api.bible
  2. Paste it below and press Enable

The key stays in your browser only. The app verifies it and adds every AMP and Spanish edition your key can access.

How this site handles the text

  • Scripture is never paraphrased. Verses load word-for-word from the translation you pick above, and the translation is always credited.
  • Commentary is labelled commentary. Historical notes, dates and themes are study helps — clearly separated from the Bible text itself.
  • Honest about debate. Where scholars disagree (dates, authorship), both views are shown instead of pretending certainty.
Open a chapter to see its historical background.

The assistant only answers from the Bible text and the built-in study notes — it never invents verses or facts.

Scripture quotations are loaded unaltered from the selected translation. Historical notes reflect mainstream scholarly and traditional views; disputed points are marked as such. Lamp & Light is a study aid, not a substitute for the Scriptures themselves.
